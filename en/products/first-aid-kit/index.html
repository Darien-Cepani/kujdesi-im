First aid kit

Everything for small cuts, burns and sprains, in one place.

  • Children
  • Hygiene

  • Home delivery. In Tirana, usually within 24 hours.

In plain words

A small bag with the dressings, gauze, antiseptic and scissors you need when someone gets a cut, a burn or a twisted ankle. Everything has its own pocket, so you find it fast when you’re in a hurry. Keep it at home or in the car.

When you need it

  • Minor cuts, grazes and burns
  • Sprains and bumps
  • Travel and homes with children

How to use it

  1. 1Keep it somewhere everyone in the house knows.
  2. 2Restock whatever you used after each use.
  3. 3Check expiry dates once a year.

Nurse’s tip

Add a card with the emergency number (127) and the medicines each person at home takes.
